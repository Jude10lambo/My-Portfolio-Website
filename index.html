01 ABOUT

A little about me

Curiosity in motion.
Ambition with direction.

I’m Jude Mike-Akwudi, a Nigerian automotive entrepreneur, community builder and digital creator, developing my career in technology. My work brings together vehicle sourcing and commerce, enthusiast culture, automotive media and technical education. I’m the founder and CEO of Miletus Motoren Werks Limited, founder and president of Torque Tribe Port Harcourt, and creator behind Miletus Auto Blog.

02 THE AUTOMOTIVE SECTOR

A personal passion

More than a machine.
A world in motion.

Automotive has always held my attention because it brings design, engineering, performance and culture together in one place.

I’m drawn to the craft behind a vehicle, the feeling of seeing one move with purpose, and the community that gathers around the road. A drift event, a carefully considered detail or a conversation between enthusiasts can each tell a different story about the relationship people have with cars.

That passion is becoming something I can share and build on. Through Miletus Auto Blog, I document automotive stories and moments on video. Through Miletus Motoren Werks, I’m connected to the business side of the sector. My goal is to keep learning the industry and find meaningful ways to contribute to its future.

FOUNDER & CEO · REGISTERED JUNE 2025

Miletus Motoren Werks Limited

Miletus is my automotive venture, built around vehicle sourcing, sales and a more personal ownership experience. I coordinate procurement, connect buyers and sellers, support negotiations and work with commission and finder’s-fee models across luxury, performance and everyday vehicles.

The wider vision is an automotive concierge and enthusiast brand: curated vehicle access, performance and customization experiences, and a distinctive Nigerian identity. My work also includes customer relationship management, lead generation, vehicle research and listings, digital content, and building relationships with dealers and service providers.
